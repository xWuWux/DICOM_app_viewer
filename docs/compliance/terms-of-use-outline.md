# Terms of use: outline

Outline for the acceptable-use agreement doctors accept before their first session. README's security
note already requires one: the watermark is an attribution and deterrence control, it cannot prevent
photographing the screen, and that limit must be stated in what participants sign. **TO DECIDE:**
wording, language (Polish), who approves it, and how acceptance is recorded (today the system records
no acceptance).

## Proposed sections
1. **Who may use the system.** Only participants who received a personal session link from the
   coordinator. Links are personal and must not be shared or forwarded (a link works for whoever
   holds it).
2. **Purpose.** Training and assessment in Lung-RADS categorisation on anonymised CT studies.
3. **Confidentiality of study material.** No photographing, screen recording, copying or
   reproducing of the studies, reports or answer key, by any means. The session shows the
   participant id and time on screen; leaked material can be traced back to the session.
4. **Technical restrictions.** Clipboard, file transfer, download, printing and the viewer's export
   functions are disabled by design; attempts to bypass them are prohibited.
5. **Exam integrity.** Answers are the participant's own work; no collaboration during the test stage
   unless the course says otherwise (**TO DECIDE**).
6. **Availability.** The service is provided as is, without availability guarantees; sessions end
   after inactivity; progress is kept on the server and resumes at the current case.
7. **Results and research use.** How results are used and published: links to the privacy notice;
   **TO DECIDE.**
8. **Breach of terms.** Consequences **TO DECIDE** (course rules).
9. **Changes and contact.** **TO DECIDE.**
